Agronomist, Field Development

Own field validation, farmer demonstrations and crop advisory across Karnataka, while turning trial data and market feedback into practical product recommendations.

Karnataka
Experience
3 to 5 years
Salary
Competitive, plus separate travel reimbursement
Type
Full Time
Reports to
State Head or Regional Sales Manager

About the employer

A growing agricultural inputs company expanding field development across Karnataka.

What you will own

  • Stay current with agronomy, crop protection and crop nutrition developments relevant to field trials and product positioning
  • Validate new formulations under field conditions and report efficacy, crop response and phytotoxicity findings to the research and development team
  • Support CRO bio efficacy programmes through protocol compliance, site selection and data quality checks
  • Run dealer and key farmer demonstration plots across launch districts
  • Collect, validate and analyse trial data, then convert findings into field ready recommendations and Packages of Practices
  • Conduct demonstrations and village meetings that educate farmers and establish product performance
  • Provide practical crop advisory for pest attacks, nutrient deficiencies and diseases
  • Support product launches and equip field sales teams with product knowledge, technical bulletins and competitor differentiation
  • Report field performance, competitor activity and pest dynamics through structured, evidence based feedback
  • Coordinate with field officers, research teams and external research partners to keep trials and market development activities on protocol

What we are looking for

  • BSc or MSc in Agriculture, preferably Agronomy, Entomology or Plant Pathology
  • 3 to 5 years of experience in agricultural inputs, crop protection, crop nutrition or biostimulants in a field development, technical or agronomy role
  • Hands on experience with field trials, product demonstrations and farmer training
  • Practical ability to diagnose pest, disease and nutrient related crop problems
  • Working knowledge of Karnataka cropping systems, including paddy, tomato, chilli and horticultural crops
  • Fluent Kannada and English communication
  • Strong discipline in trial data collection, analysis and reporting
  • Own two wheeler, valid driving licence and willingness to travel extensively across rural Karnataka

Nice to have

  • Experience supporting new agricultural product launches
  • Experience working with key farmers, dealers, field sales teams or CRO partners

How we will assess you

Every application is read against a scorecard agreed with the hiring manager, not filtered on keywords. If your experience fits the role but your CV does not use the expected words, we would still rather see it. A recruiter reviews every shortlist, and no candidate is ever rejected by software alone.

You will hear back either way. If you are not right for this role we will tell you, rather than leaving you wondering.
