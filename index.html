Our AI

Three places AI does the heavy lifting

Recruitment has always been limited by how many profiles one person can realistically read in a day. AI removes that ceiling, so our recruiters spend their hours on judgement instead of triage.

01 / SOURCING

AI sourcing

We search on meaning, not keywords. Our AI reads a role the way a hiring manager would, then finds people whose actual experience matches, including strong candidates whose CV never uses your exact terminology.

  • Semantic matching on capability
  • Job boards, our database, professional networks
  • Reaches passive candidates who never applied
02 / SCREENING

AI screening

Every profile gets read, not just the first fifty. Our AI parses each CV against the scorecard we agreed at intake, ranks candidates, and writes out the reasoning, including the gaps and the inconsistencies worth asking about.

  • Scored against your criteria, not a generic template
  • Written rationale for every ranking
  • Flags career gaps, job hopping, credential mismatches
03 / ASSESSMENT

AI assessment

Role specific assessments that test whether someone can do the work, scored against the same rubric for every candidate. No panel fatigue, no first impression bias, no difference between the person interviewed on Monday and the one on Friday.

  • Technical and functional skill tests
  • Consistent rubric across the whole pool
  • Comparable scores you can defend internally

A human always signs off

No candidate is rejected by software alone. AI narrows and ranks, a recruiter reviews, and every shortlist that reaches you has been read and argued for by a person who understands your team. We will also tell you when the AI and the recruiter disagree, because that is usually the most useful signal in the whole process.

Our approach

AI handles the volume. Culture fit needs a human.

Technical evaluation is close to a solved problem now. Between AI screening, structured assessments and panel rounds, most companies can tell whether someone is able to do the job.

What still goes unmeasured is everything else. How a person handles disagreement, whether they need structure or resent it, how they behave when a deadline slips, whether they are actually motivated by the work in front of them or just by leaving their last job. That is where hires quietly fail, six months in, at full cost to you.

So we let AI do what it is good at, reading everything and ranking it consistently, and we spend the time it frees up on the part that needs a person. We build a picture of how your team really operates, not the values on the wall but the actual norms, and we interview against it. Every shortlist includes a written view on fit, reservations included. You get an honest read, not a sales pitch.

How it works

Four steps, no black box

You always know where the search stands, why a candidate is on the list, and what the AI scored them on.

Intake

We sit with the hiring manager to understand the role, the team it joins, and what good looks like here specifically, then turn that into the scorecard the AI screens against.

AI sourcing

Semantic search across job boards, our database and professional networks, plus targeted outreach. We go after people doing the work, not only people applying for it.

Screen & assess

AI screening and role specific assessment, then a structured culture fit interview with a recruiter. You get a shortlist with scores and a written rationale for each person.

Close

We manage scheduling, feedback loops, offer negotiation and the notice period, then stay in touch through onboarding to protect the joining.
